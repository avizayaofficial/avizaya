The Four Pillars

A woman broken in any one of these
is never fully free.

I

Spirit

Identity and purpose. She knows who she is before God — not borrowed from a relationship, a title or a following.

II

Body

Healing and embodiment. She lives in her body, not at war with it. The vessel through which everything else flows.

III

Mind

Discipline and becoming. An inner architecture that holds her on the hard days. Her habits are her devotion.

IV

Wealth

Freedom and legacy. She builds systems, not just income — the freedom to live her calling without permission.
