School 1: The abandoned girl  ·  Episode 12  ·  Finale

Abandonment is your alchemy.

The closing teaching of this school, where every wound becomes the very ingredient God uses to make you unbreakable.

Welcome to the finale

You have walked through eleven episodes to arrive at this one. You are not the same woman who began this school. Whether you can feel the change yet or not, the very fact that you have come this far means the Lord has been working inside you, and the work is real, and the work is permanent, and the woman who is reading these words today is a different woman than the one who first opened Episode 1.

This is the closing teaching of the school, and what it offers you is the alchemy of your own story. What if I told you that the deepest wound you have carried, the one that made you feel invisible, unworthy and left behind, is the exact ingredient the Lord has been using to make you unbreakable? What if the very thing you have been trying to escape your whole life is also the very thing He has been forming into the substance of your calling?

That is what alchemy is. The ancient alchemists believed that base metal could be turned into gold through the right combination of fire, pressure and time. They were not wrong about the principle. They were wrong about the elements. The Lord has been the true alchemist all along, and what He has been transmuting is not metal but the very pain of His daughters into the very gold of their purpose. The pain was not wasted. The pain was the raw material. And the gold is what He has been making out of it the whole time.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

Romans 8:28 NIV

Paul names the spiritual principle behind the alchemy. The Father takes all things, even the things that look like destruction, even the seasons that look like waste, and He weaves them together for the good of His daughter and for the working out of the purpose He set over her life before the foundation of the world. This is not the empty promise the world makes that everything will be fine. This is the deep promise of a Father who is so capable that there is no piece of your story, no matter how painful, that He cannot use as raw material for the woman He is forming you to become.

Part 1

Pain is inevitable. Suffering is optional.

The reality of life is that pain is inevitable. Every woman, no matter how protected or privileged or faithful, will be touched by pain somewhere in her journey. The wounds are part of the human inheritance, and the Lord did not promise to keep her from them. He promised something better than the absence of pain. He promised to be with her in the middle of it, and to make of it something she would not have been able to make of it on her own.

Pain is inevitable, but suffering is optional. The distinction matters. Pain is what happens to you. Suffering is what you do with what happened. Two women can walk through identical wounds and arrive at completely different destinations, and the difference between them is not the wound itself, it is what they did with the wound once it had been given to them. One let it bury her. The other let it build her. The pain was the same. The outcome was opposite.

The daughter of God is not asked to pretend the pain did not happen. She is not asked to skip the grief. She is not asked to deny what was hard or unfair or undeserved. She is asked something different. She is asked to take the pain into the hands of the Father and to let Him show her what to do with it next. The suffering, the unending replay of what was done to her, the bitterness that takes up residence in her when the wound goes unprocessed, that is the part she can choose to walk out of. The Lord has been holding out the doorway for years. This episode is the work of finally walking through it.

The principle

Pain is what happened to you. Suffering is what you have been doing with what happened. The first is unavoidable. The second is the part the Lord is asking you to release into His hands today.

Part 2

The three-step alchemy.

The Lord gave me a practice for transmuting pain into power, and it is the practice that has carried me through every difficult season of my life since the day I learned it. It is not theory. It is the very work I have done with my own hands, in my own kitchen, in my own quiet hours with God. I am giving it to you now in three movements, because every daughter of God needs a structured way to do the inner work of alchemy, and these three steps have been the most reliable structure I have found.

Step one

Stop resisting the pain.

Pain demands to be felt. You cannot transmute what you have not first acknowledged, and the great mistake of so much modern teaching has been to skip over the feeling in pursuit of the healing. The skipping does not work. The pain you do not feel does not disappear, it goes underground, and it operates from underneath your life in ways you cannot see, shaping decisions and reactions and relationships from a hidden place.

Acknowledge the pain. Do not ignore it or try to numb it. Face it. Write it down. Speak it aloud. Sit with it in the presence of the Lord, the way Mary sat at the feet of Jesus while Martha was busy in the other room. Denial keeps you stuck. Acceptance is the first step toward transformation.

There is a deeper principle inside this practice that the daughter of God needs to see, because the naming of what hurts you is not just acknowledgment, it is dominion. When Adam was placed in the garden, the very first work the Lord gave him was to name the animals, and the naming was the establishment of his authority over them. When Jesus encountered demons, He commanded them to give Him their name before He cast them out, and the naming was the act of authority that preceded the casting. The pattern is consistent across all of Scripture. To name a thing is to take dominion over it. What you cannot name continues to rule you from the shadows. What you can name comes under your authority the moment you speak it.

So when you sit with your pain and name it, when you write down exactly what hurt you and exactly how, when you speak the names of what was done and what was felt and what was lost, you are not wallowing. You are exercising dominion. You are doing the very first work the Father gave to Adam in the garden, and the very work Jesus did with every spirit He confronted. The naming is the beginning of the authority. Do not fear the naming. The naming is what makes the next two steps possible.

Step two

Ask what the pain has been teaching you.

Once the pain has been acknowledged, the question shifts. Not why did this happen, which is the question that keeps a woman trapped in the unresolvable mystery of suffering, but what is this teaching me, which is the question that begins the alchemy. Realize that your struggles are not your enemy. They are your curriculum. They are part of the training process through which the Lord has been forming a greater version of yourself, and the school you have been enrolled in is the school of becoming the woman He always meant for you to be.

Every warrior has a first wound. You are not the exception. Take responsibility for taking your life into your own hands, with the Lord beside you, and begin to rewrite the narrative from victim to victor. The narrative you have been telling yourself about what happened to you is the narrative that is keeping you where you are. A new narrative is the door to a new life. The same events. A different woman telling them. That is alchemy.

Step three

Channel the energy forward.

Pain is energy. The same energy that has been weighing you down can become the very fuel that lifts you up, but only if you channel it forward. Take your pain and convert it into fuel to train harder, create more boldly, build with more conviction, serve other women who hurt like you did. Power is not the absence of pain. Power is the courage to alchemize pain into purpose.

The very pain that was meant to weaken and destroy you can be the same pain that empowers you and lifts you to a higher dimension of yourself. At the end of every day, pain is just energy, and you get to decide where it goes. Will you let it destroy you, or will you transmute it into power? The choice, Darling, is yours, and it is a choice the Lord has been waiting for you to make for years.

The inversion

You were not left behind. You were set up.

There is a single sentence that, when the daughter of God finally receives it, ends the long inner story she has been telling herself about why her life began the way it did, and replaces that story with the truth.

You were not left behind. You were set up for a comeback. The very wound that has been telling you for years that you were forgotten is the very ingredient the Lord has been using to write the greatest story of your life.

Every comeback in Scripture began with a setup. Joseph was thrown into a pit before he ran a nation. David was hunted across the wilderness before he became king. Esther was orphaned before she saved her people. Mary was a young unmarried girl in a small town before she carried the Son of God. The pattern is consistent. The setup looks like an ending and the comeback looks like a miracle, but the miracle was always the plan. The setup was always part of the architecture.

You have been calling your beginning a tragedy, and the Lord has been calling it a setup. The two readings of the same events produce two completely different women on the other side of them. The woman who believes she was left behind walks through her life apologizing for being here. The woman who believes she was set up for a comeback walks through her life with the quiet authority of one who knows what she has overcome and what she is becoming. The choice between these two women is the choice this episode is putting in front of you.

Part 3

The kintsugi woman.

There is an ancient Japanese art called kintsugi, which means golden joinery. When a precious piece of pottery breaks, the kintsugi master does not throw the pottery away. He does not hide the cracks. He takes the broken pieces and rejoins them with veins of gold, so that the very fractures become a place of beauty in the vessel. The repaired bowl is more valuable than the unbroken bowl ever was, because the gold of its repair tells the story of what it survived, and the survival is the very thing that makes it sacred.

The daughter of God who has walked this entire school is a kintsugi woman. The fractures of her story are not the disqualifying scars she has been embarrassed by for years. The fractures are the very places where the gold of the Lord has been poured in, slowly, faithfully, season by season, until what was broken became something more beautiful than it would ever have been without the breaking. The wounds are not erased in the work of healing. The wounds are illuminated. The gold runs through them. And the woman who emerges from the work is more beautiful than the untouched version of herself could ever have been, because the gold is the testimony, and the testimony is the very thing that points to the Father who did the joining.

This is what the Word of God means when it names her a new creation. Not a woman with the past erased. A woman with the past redeemed. The fractures are still there, but they have been filled with the gold of His faithfulness, and the woman walking out of this school is the kintsugi version of the woman who walked into it.

Part 4

From ice to diamond.

Years ago, in the heart of my own transformation, I wrote a line in a song that became a personal truth I have lived inside ever since. I sang, “Your endless love transformed my heart from ice to diamond.” I did not know, when I wrote it, how complete the transformation would become. I only knew that I had been one thing when I came into His presence, and I had become another thing after I had been with Him long enough.

Ice and diamond are made of the same essence. The difference between them is what the substance has been through. Ice is water that has been still. Diamond is carbon that has been through unimaginable pressure for an unimaginable length of time. The pressure is not the enemy of the diamond. The pressure is the very thing that made it. And the same is true of the daughter of God who has been through the fire and the storm and the long faithful work of the Spirit forming her into who He has always seen her becoming.

You have been under pressure. You have been through fire. You have been in seasons that felt like they would crush you, and the seasons did not crush you, they compressed you into something the world has never seen before. You are the diamond version of who you were when this school began. The pressure was the very thing that made you. And the brilliance you carry now is not the brilliance of the untouched. It is the brilliance of the daughter who has been through everything, and come out on the other side carrying the very light of God in places no untouched woman could have carried it.

Part 5

For such a time as this.

There is a teaching the Word of God gives that the daughter of God needs to receive at the close of this school, because it shifts the entire weight of the work she has been doing. The healing she has been receiving is not just for her. The healing has corporate implications. Her personal victory becomes a corporate breakthrough, and the very wounds that almost broke her are the very wounds the Lord is going to use to reach the women coming after her.

The clearest picture of this principle in all of Scripture is the story of Esther. She was an orphan girl in a foreign land. Her parents were dead. Her people were exiled. She had been raised by her older cousin Mordecai because there was no one else to raise her, and her early life was marked by the very kinds of wounds that mark so many daughters of God in our own day. The Lord brought her, through a series of events she did not engineer and could not have predicted, into the position of queen of Persia, and from that position she discovered that her entire people were about to be exterminated by a royal decree she had not been told about.

Mordecai sent word to her that she had to act, and her first response was fear. She told him she could not approach the king uninvited, that to do so could cost her life. And Mordecai sent back to her the sentence that has become the call of every daughter of God who has ever wondered whether her wounds and her position were given to her for a reason.

"And who knows but that you have come to your royal position for such a time as this?"

Esther 4:14 NIV

For such a time as this. Esther was placed where she was placed, with the story she had, at the exact moment her people needed her, because the Lord had been writing her life as a deliverance for someone other than herself the whole time. The orphan girl was the only one who could save the nation, and the very thing that had qualified her was the very wound the world had counted against her from the beginning.

Here is the deeper truth of the Esther story that the daughter of God needs to receive at the close of this school. Esther saved her people because she was a Jew. She understood her people from the inside, in a way no Persian could have understood them, no matter how well-meaning. She knew their God. She knew their fears. She knew what was at stake for them, because what was at stake for them was also at stake for her. Her ability to save them was directly proportional to the fact that she was one of them.

The same is true of you. The women the Lord is going to send to you in the years ahead are the women whose stories will rhyme with yours. The woman who has been through abandonment will be sent to you, and you will be able to reach her in a way no untouched woman could ever reach her, because you will understand her from the inside. The woman who has been in the wrong relationship, the woman who has been carrying the orphan spirit, the woman who has been unable to forgive herself, every one of them will be reachable by you specifically because of what you survived. Your survival qualified you. Your wounds became the very credentials that allow you to walk into their pain and bring the Lord into the room with you.

You have not been suffering for nothing. You have been being trained as a deliverer. The very seasons you have spent in the dark were the seasons the Lord was preparing you to be the woman who walks into the dark room of another woman’s life and turns on the light. You are an Esther. You were placed where you were placed, with the story you have, for such a time as this. The orphan girl of yesterday is the queen who is being asked to act on behalf of the women still trapped where she used to be.

You have not been healing alone. You have been healing on behalf of a generation that is yet to come. The work you have done in these twelve episodes is not just for the woman you see in the mirror. It is for the women in your bloodline who will be healed because you broke a generational pattern. It is for the women in your circle who will see the change in you and ask what you have been doing. It is for the women the Lord will send to you in the years ahead, the ones who do not yet know your name, who will need exactly the depth of compassion that only your particular path of healing could have produced in you.

This is why the school cannot end with you simply feeling better. This is why the healing must move into action. The Lord does not heal His daughters in a vacuum. He heals them so they can become the very evidence to the next woman that healing is possible. Your story is hers in advance. The same alchemy that has been working in you is the alchemy you are now equipped to carry to her.

Part 6

The promise He left for you.

There is a verse in Habakkuk that has been my favorite verse in the entire Bible for as long as I have walked with the Lord, and I am giving it to you now as the promise He has been holding over your life from the beginning.

"Look at the nations and watch, and be utterly amazed. For I am going to do something in your days that you would not believe even if you were told."

Habakkuk 1:5 NIV

The Lord is going to do something in your days that you would not believe even if you were told. Read that again. He is not asking you to figure out what it is. He is not asking you to engineer it. He is asking you to position yourself in the place where He can do it, and that place is the very position you have been being formed in across the last eleven episodes. The position of a daughter who has stopped abandoning herself. The position of a daughter who knows whose she is. The position of a daughter whose healing is real, ongoing, deepening, and ready to be released into the next chapter of her life.

"Call to me and I will answer you and tell you great and unsearchable things you do not know."

Jeremiah 33:3 NIV

Jeremiah names the practice that pairs with the Habakkuk promise. Call on Him. Ask. Listen. He will answer with things you have not yet known. The things He has been preparing for you are great and unsearchable, which means they are beyond what your mind has been able to imagine on its own. The very fact that you cannot imagine what He is about to do is part of the evidence that He is about to do it. He works in the realm of the unimaginable, and your job is to keep showing up in the place where He can reach you.

Part 7

The paradox of the wound.

There is a paradox at the heart of every abandonment wound that the daughter of God needs to see clearly before this school closes, because the paradox is the very thing that has been keeping her trapped, and the seeing of it is the beginning of being free from it.

The paradox is this. If she does not heal from the abandonment, she ends up alone. Not because life is cruel, but because the very wound she refused to release begins to operate from inside her, and it slowly pushes away the very people who could have loved her. The trust issues, the bracing, the testing of every relationship, the assuming the worst, the leaving before she can be left, all of it is the residue of an unhealed wound that has been keeping watch for the next betrayal, even when there is no betrayal coming. The wound that began as an injury done to her becomes the architecture of her own continued aloneness, and the architecture goes on building itself for years if she does not consent to the work of taking it down.

This is what unforgiveness does, in its quiet, slow, undetected way. The spirit that wounded her, when she refuses to release it, finds a home inside her, and it begins to operate her life from the inside out. She starts to push away the closest people. She starts to mistrust the safest ones. She starts to recreate, with new faces, the very abandonment she has been trying to avoid, because the unhealed wound has been generating its own evidence, and the evidence is what her nervous system has been bracing for the whole time. The original wound was not her fault. The continued recreation of it, after she has been given the tools to release it, is now part of the work she is responsible for.

There is a small experiment from the natural world that illustrates the principle better than any teaching can. Two jars of rice are set out on a shelf. One jar is spoken kindly to every day, given attention, given care. The other is left in a corner, ignored, neglected, given no contact. Within weeks, the neglected jar of rice begins to mold and decay, while the cared-for jar remains intact. This experiment has been replicated in different settings with different substances, and the principle holds. What is left alone, what is denied connection, what is treated as if it does not matter, begins to break down. We are not designed to be alone. We are not designed to be neglected. The wound of abandonment carries within it a biological truth, that the human soul, when it is not received by anyone, begins to decay in the way the rice did, and the only thing that reverses the decay is being received again, in love, by Someone who has been waiting to receive her all along.

The Father has been that Someone for you the whole time. He has been receiving you in the moments no one else was. He has been speaking kindness over you in the silences when no one else was speaking. He has been keeping you intact through seasons that would have decayed you completely if He had not been there. And the work of this school has been the work of letting yourself finally be received by Him, fully, the way the cared-for jar of rice was received by the daily kindness of the one tending it. The reception is the very thing that has been keeping you whole, and the reception is what will continue keeping you whole, for the rest of your life, as long as you keep returning to it.

There is a sentence I have come to live by, in my own walk with these truths, and I am giving it to you now. People fear abandonment more than they fear the truth. Most of the dynamics that have been costing daughters of God their peace for years have been built around the fear of being left, and the fear has caused them to hide, to perform, to shrink, to settle, to stay silent, to tolerate the intolerable, all to avoid the one outcome they were most afraid of. The Lord wants His daughters free from that fear, because He has already settled the question. He will not leave them. The fear of abandonment, in the heart of a daughter who has been claimed by her Father, is a fear that has no current evidence to stand on, and the laying down of it is the very freedom this school has been forming.

The paradox resolves the moment she chooses to receive what He has been holding out to her. The unforgiveness laid down. The wound released. The Father received as the One who has been with her all along. The aloneness ends. The connection deepens. The very people the wound was pushing away begin to be drawn closer, because the woman they are now meeting is the woman the Lord has been forming, not the woman the wound had been operating, and the difference is felt before it is understood.

Part 8

The bridge to what comes next.

The work of School 1 has been the work of the spirit. Of identity. Of who you are before the Father, before your roles, before your wounds, before the world had a word to say about you. The spiritual ground has been laid. The roots have gone down. The daughter spirit has been received. And the next chapter of your formation is going to take everything you have just received in the spirit, and begin to bring it into the very body that has been carrying you through it all.

You have a body, and that body has been carrying everything for years. The grief, the exhaustion, the suppressed emotion, the hypervigilance, the addictions, the loneliness. Your body has been the vessel that has been holding it all, and most of the work of School 1 has been happening above the body, in the realm of belief and identity and forgiveness. Now the work has to move down. The healing must come into the vessel that has been carrying you, because a healed spirit in a depleted body is only half of the woman God has been forming, and the other half is coming.

Coming next

The body temple.

Your body is not your enemy. It is your vessel. And a healed spirit is meant to inhabit a body that is being honored, nourished and tended as the sacred dwelling the Father designed it to be.

The body has been speaking to you the whole time. The exhaustion has been speaking. The tension has been speaking. The way you have been eating, or not eating, has been speaking. The way you have or have not been moving your body has been speaking. The way you have been sleeping has been speaking. And the body has been telling you the truth, because the body has been the meeting place between the wound and the woman the whole time, and the wound cannot complete its healing without the body it has been living in being included in the work.

The next school will take everything you have received here and move it into the vessel. The same Spirit that has been forming your inner life will begin to form the way you live in the body. The same alchemy that has been turning your wound into gold will begin to turn your physical neglect into stewardship, your physical exhaustion into restoration, your physical disconnection into the holy connection between a daughter and the temple her Father has given her to live inside. The journey continues. The work deepens. And the woman who walked into School 1 abandoned is the woman who walks into School 2 sacred, embodied and ready.

A brief note

On my own walk.

I built this school out of the very ground my own life was rebuilt on. There is not a single principle in any of these twelve episodes that I did not first walk through myself, in seasons that almost broke me, in years that I now look back on as the very years the Lord was forming the woman who would one day be able to teach these things to you.

For many years I asked God why. Why did my family carry what we carried. Why was I born into the story I was born into. Why did the wounds of my early life have to be so deep. I asked Him over and over again, and I did not get an answer to the question why, because why was not the right question. The right question was what. What is He preparing me for. What is He forming in me. What is the gold He is putting in the broken places. The moment I learned to ask what instead of why, the alchemy began. The same wounds that had been my torment became my curriculum. The same story that had been my shame became my testimony. The same pain that had been my prison became my purpose.

You are now standing where I was standing the day the alchemy began for me. You have the principles. You have the practices. You have the vocabulary. You have a Father who has been waiting on this very moment for the whole of your life. There is no more delay. The healing has been activated. The new chapter has begun.

Five questions for the close of this school

Read one a day this week. Sit with it. Let it bring up everything you have learned across the twelve episodes.

One. If you were to write the new narrative of your life, the one where you were set up for a comeback instead of left behind, what would the opening sentence be?

Two. Of the three steps of alchemy in Part 2, which one is most ready to be put into practice in your life this week, and what would that look like in concrete daily action?

Three. Which of the women in your bloodline, your circle, or your future will be healed because you walked through this school? Hold their faces in your mind as you continue to walk this out.

Four. What is the unimaginable thing the Lord might be preparing to do in your days, and what would it cost you to begin to position yourself for it now?

Five. What is one piece of the inner work of this school that you know is not yet finished, and what is the next faithful step the Lord is asking you to take into it?
