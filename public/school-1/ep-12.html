School 1: The Abandoned Girl  ·  Episode 12

Abandonment is your alchemy.

You came in here as the abandoned girl. You are leaving as the woman who stopped abandoning herself.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

Romans 8:28 NIV  ·  Anchor verse
Before we begin

We have come a long way together in this school. You have looked at things that most people spend their entire lives avoiding. And I want to honour that before we close.

This last episode is not about a new wound. It is about what you do with everything you now know. Because understanding your wounds without actually changing is just sophisticated suffering. The knowledge is not the destination. The knowledge is the map. The destination is a different life.

Part 1

What alchemy means

Alchemy is the ancient practice of transforming base metals into gold. But the true alchemy was never about the metal. It was about the transformation of the person who worked the process.

The pain you have been carrying is not just a wound to be healed. It is raw material. It is base metal waiting to be turned into something that could not exist without the fire.

What you went through is your curriculum. Not your identity, not your limit. Your curriculum. Every single thing that happened to you, every abandonment, every wound, every pattern you developed to survive, it equipped you to understand something about the human experience that people who did not go through it cannot access. And that understanding is your greatest asset in building a life that matters.

"And we know that in all things God works for the good of those who love him, who have been called according to his purpose."

Romans 8:28 NIV
Part 2

Your pain is someone else's door

There is a woman somewhere right now who is where you were two years ago. She is in the eating disorder, or the trauma bond, or the three o'clock in the morning searching for something that will make the pain stop. She cannot find her way out because she does not yet know that out is possible.

And the person most qualified to show her is someone who has been exactly there. Not a therapist who studied it. Not a pastor who read about it. Someone who was in it, who understands it from the inside, who can say I know what that place feels like and I also know that you do not have to stay there.

That person is you.

"Your wound is your qualification. Your survival is your testimony. Your past is God's proof of His grace. Your story is God's story of love."

Part 3

What you carry out of this school

You carry this: your identity does not belong to what was done to you. You were known by God before any of it happened. The abandonment did not define you. The addiction did not define you. The wrong relationships did not define you. They were things that happened to a person who was always already more than those things.

You carry this: the healing is not a destination. It is a daily practice of choosing yourself. Of staying at your own table. Of keeping the small promises that expand your self-worth. Of going to the spring of living water instead of the broken cistern.

And you carry this: what was meant to destroy you has become your authority. Not despite the wound. Because of it. The kintsugi vessel is not less beautiful for having been broken. It is more beautiful. The cracks are where the gold went in.

"They triumphed over him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony."

Revelation 12:11 NIV

By the word of their testimony. Your story is a weapon. Not against other people, against the silence that keeps other women from knowing what is possible. Every time you speak what you have survived, you are handing someone else the key to their own freedom.

Questions to sit with this week

One. Looking back over this school, what is the single most important thing you have understood about yourself?

Two. What has changed in you since you began? Even one small thing. Name it.

Three. Who is the woman you are becoming? Not who you were. Who you are becoming.

Four. Who is already in your life who needs what you now carry?

Five. What is one commitment you are making to yourself as you leave this school and enter the next?

A prayer for this episode

God, thank You. For the wounds that became wisdom. For the pain that became purpose. For the years that felt lost and that You are restoring.

I am not who I was when I started this school. And I am not yet who I am becoming. I am in the middle. And I am choosing to stay.

Use everything. Every single thing. The eating disorder. The suicidal years. The wrong relationships. The fear. The shame. Use it all. Let none of it be wasted.

I am your alchemy. Turn it to gold.

Amen.

You came in here as the abandoned girl.
You are leaving as the woman who stopped abandoning herself.
And that changes everything that comes after this.
