School 1: The Abandoned Girl  ·  Episode 10

The orphan spirit and how to break free.

You are not an orphan scraping for approval. You are an heir receiving what was always yours.

"The Spirit you received does not make you slaves, so that you live in fear again; rather, the Spirit you received brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, 'Abba, Father.'"

Romans 8:15 NIV  ·  Anchor verse
Before we begin

The orphan spirit is not about being physically abandoned as a child. It is about the internal posture of someone who has never fully received the security of being unconditionally claimed.

You can grow up with both parents and carry an orphan spirit. You can be surrounded by people who love you and still feel fundamentally alone. You can be in a room full of people and feel like no one actually knows you. That is the orphan spirit. And it is one of the most common and least named wounds in the church today.

Part 1

What the orphan spirit is

The orphan spirit is the internal operating system of a person who has not yet received the revelation that they are fully, unconditionally, permanently loved by God. It is not an intellectual deficit. You can know the theology and still not have received the reality.

The orphan spirit functions on fear, on scarcity, on the deep background hum that says: I have to earn my place. If I stop performing, I will be removed. If I am truly known, I will be left.

The orphan spirit makes you a competitor rather than a collaborator. It makes generosity hard because resources feel scarce. It makes rest feel dangerous because worth is measured by productivity. It makes intimacy terrifying because being fully known means being fully available to be rejected.

"The Spirit you received does not make you slaves, so that you live in fear again; rather, the Spirit you received brought about your adoption to sonship. And by him we cry, 'Abba, Father.'"

Romans 8:15 NIV
Part 2

The spirit of adoption

The opposite of the orphan spirit is not confidence. It is not success and it is not a healed childhood. It is adoption. It is the experiential, not just theological, reality of being claimed by a Father who never changes His mind about you.

Abba. That is an Aramaic word. It is the word a small child uses for their father. Not the formal word, the intimate one. The one used in the middle of the night when you wake up scared, the one used when you run to the safe person without thinking.

God is that Abba. And the spirit of adoption means that you get to run to Him that way, without formality, without earning your way in first. Just the running, just the cry, just the arms that were already open before you arrived.

"All these years I was waiting for God to change my life while God was waiting for me to become willing to change."

Part 3

From orphan to heir

"Now if we are children, then we are heirs — heirs of God and co-heirs with Christ."

Romans 8:17 NIV

An orphan scrapes for what they need. An heir receives what has already been prepared. An orphan performs to justify their presence. An heir belongs before they do anything. An orphan lives in fear of being removed. An heir rests in the security of a covenant that cannot be revoked.

This is not just beautiful language. It is a description of two entirely different internal operating systems. And the one you are currently running determines every single thing about how you move through the world.

The healing of the abandonment wound is, at its deepest level, the journey from orphan to heir. From performing for a place to resting in one that was already given.

Questions to sit with this week

One. Where does the orphan spirit show up most strongly in your life? In relationships? In work? In how you receive love?

Two. Do you have the theology of being God's child without the experiential reality of it? What is the gap?

Three. What would change in your daily life if you operated from the posture of an heir rather than an orphan?

Four. When was the last time you cried Abba to God? Not a formal prayer. Just the running?

Five. What is one area where you have been scraping when you were actually meant to receive?

A prayer for this episode

Abba. Father. I say those words and I want to mean them all the way down. Not just in my mind. In my body. In the part of me that still braces for the leaving.

Replace the orphan spirit in me with the spirit of adoption. Let me know, in the way that changes how I move through the world, that I am claimed. That I am permanent. That there is nothing I can do to be unclaimed.

Teach me to receive. Teach me to rest. Teach me what it feels like to belong before I have earned anything.

Amen.

You are not an orphan scraping for approval.
You are an heir receiving what was always yours.
The Father never changed His mind about you.
